# Fire-Protection-System-Design

## Overview

This project presents the **design of a conventional fire protection system for Khan Jahan Ali Hall, KUET**, covering the South Building's 1st and 2nd floors.

The design integrates **fire detection, alarm notification, automatic sprinkler suppression, portable fire extinguishers, and safe means of escape** into a coordinated life-safety system. The project also includes system component selection, technical specifications, AutoCAD-based layout design, cost estimation, and justification according to **Bangladesh National Building Code (BNBC)** and relevant **NFPA standards**.

## Objectives

* Design a fire protection system for the selected residential building.
* Identify the required fire detection, alarm, and suppression equipment.
* Develop the system layout using **AutoCAD**.
* Estimate the overall project cost.
* Justify the design based on **BNBC and NFPA guidelines**.
* Develop practical understanding of professional fire protection system design.

## System Overview

The proposed system is a **conventional/zonal fire protection system** designed for a residential/hostel occupancy.

The major subsystems include:

1. Fire detection system
2. Fire alarm system
3. Automatic sprinkler system
4. Portable fire extinguishers
5. Emergency means of escape
6. Fire water supply and piping
7. Fire Alarm Control Panel (FACP)

The system is designed so that fire detection signals are transmitted to the central FACP, which then activates the appropriate alarm devices. Automatic sprinklers provide fire suppression, while extinguishers and clearly defined escape routes provide additional protection.

## Fire Detection System

**Photoelectric smoke detectors** were selected as the primary detection devices because they are suitable for residential and sleeping areas.

For the two floors:

* **44 smoke detectors**
* 22 detectors per floor
* Maximum coverage considered: approximately 100 m² per detector
* Detectors installed on ceilings
* No detectors placed inside bathrooms/showers to minimize false alarms caused by steam

The design also includes **6 manual call points**, positioned near exits and along corridors.

## Fire Alarm System

The alarm system consists of:

* 8-zone Fire Alarm Control Panel
* Manual Call Points
* Strobe sirens
* 24 V DC battery backup
* Supervised detector loops

The FACP functions as the central control unit of the system. The floors are divided into zones so that the panel can identify the affected zone when a fire detection device is activated.

### Fire Alarm Control Panel Specifications

* 8 zones
* Style-B
* 1.25 A NAC power
* 24 V DC supply
* Silence / non-silence / auto-silence modes
* Programmable duration: 5–20 minutes

## Automatic Sprinkler System

A **wet-pipe automatic sprinkler system** was incorporated for automatic fire suppression.

### Main piping

| Component      |              Size |
| -------------- | ----------------: |
| Branch line    |    1 inch / 25 mm |
| Cross main     | 1.25 inch / 32 mm |
| Vertical riser |    2 inch / 50 mm |

The sprinkler system includes:

* Sprinkler heads
* Zone control valves
* Alarm check valve
* Water flow switch
* Inspector's test connection
* Main drain valve
* Pressure gauges
* Tamper switches

A total of **156 sprinkler heads** were considered for the two floors.

## Fire Extinguishers

The design uses **5 kg ABC dry chemical powder fire extinguishers**.

A total of **14 extinguishers** were considered, positioned at staircases and at intervals consistent with the specified travel-distance requirements.

## Cabling

Two major cable types were considered:

### Detector Loop

* 1.5 mm²
* 2-core / 4-core
* FRLS PVC insulation
* 300/500 V or 450/750 V rating
* Supervised 24 V DC loop

### Sounder Circuit

* 2.5 mm²
* 2-core
* FRLS PVC insulation
* 300/500 V or 450/750 V rating

FRLS cabling was selected to provide fire-resistant and low-smoke characteristics.

## Cost Estimation

The estimated material cost for the fire protection system was:

**Material Cost: BDT 443,446**

Additional allowances were considered for:

* Installation & labour: 15%
* Testing & commissioning: 5%
* Contingency: 5%

Therefore:

**Total additional allowance: 25%**

### Estimated Project Cost

**≈ BDT 554,308 (~5.5 Lakhs BDT)**

* **NFPA 13 – Standard for the Installation of Sprinkler Systems**
* **NFPA 72 – National Fire Alarm and Signaling Code**
* **NFPA 10 – Standard for Portable Fire Extinguishers**
* **NFPA 101 – Life Safety Code**
